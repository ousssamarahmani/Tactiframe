# Tactivision.ai Public Content Audit

This audit separates verified Tactivision content from unsupported reference claims. The UI may borrow interaction patterns such as a command-centre hero, product navigator, methodology strip, and editorial sections, but it must not borrow fictional products, numbers, testimonials, logos, or performance claims.

| Public UI area | Verified basis | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opposition Analyst | One-pager describes an autonomous opposition agent and pre-match reporting around opponent intelligence. | Keep the product. Use formations, pressure triggers, recurring patterns, tactical maps, and match-ready briefing language. Avoid presenting the product as a proven production service. |
| TactiScout | User clarification and product documents describe a computer-vision AI agent for academies and smaller clubs that identifies and develops players technically, tactically, physically, and mentally. | Keep this positioning. Do not describe TactiScout as a recruitment marketplace, scouting platform, or generic shortlist generator. |
| Vision Engine | CV architecture and roadmap describe a forthcoming computer-vision layer that turns match video into structured football intelligence. | Keep as coming soon / in development. Avoid claiming universal league coverage or deployed performance. |
| Multi-agent system | The one-pager describes five specialized AI agents. | Use this as a high-level product-system fact. Do not invent individual capabilities beyond the supplied documents. |
| Product count | The current public narrative focuses on Opposition Analyst, TactiScout, and Vision Engine. | Use “three products in focus” rather than inventing a maturity or customer count. |
| Proof and social signals | No verified public customer reviews, ratings, logos, or performance results were supplied for the landing page. | Do not add them. Keep CTAs, product states, and early-access language transparent. |
| Demo flow | The site provides a contact path for a focused product conversation. | Use “focused session” and “discovery” language instead of claiming a fixed meeting duration. |

## Removed or softened claims

The UI no longer uses the unsupported “48h before kick-off” promise, “24/7 autonomous analysis” metric, “one decisive edge” proof statement, “every league” coverage claim, or “available now” product status. These may be revisited only when product evidence and public launch status support them.

## Source

Primary source reviewed: `TactiVision_One_Pager_2026.docx`, supplied by the project owner. TactiScout positioning also follows the owner’s clarification in the project conversation and the supplied CV/product architecture documents.
