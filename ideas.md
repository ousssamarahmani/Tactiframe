# Tactivision.ai — Landing Page Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### 1. Precision on the Pitch
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark editorial sportstech experience that feels like a high-performance tactical command centre. It uses disciplined field geometry, exact typography, and sharp data cues to make advanced football intelligence feel immediate.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Stadium Editorial
**Very Brief Intro:** A bright, magazine-inspired brand world where football storytelling meets premium research publishing. It builds trust with generous white space, oversized type, and photography-led momentum.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Signal Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained monochrome product laboratory where micro-green signals punctuate calm, analytical layouts. It positions the platform as an elegant intelligence system rather than a generic software dashboard.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Precision on the Pitch

### Design Movement
Contemporary sports broadcast graphics combined with Swiss editorial precision. The page will look more like an elite club’s analysis room than a conventional SaaS website.

### Core Principles
1. Make complexity feel decisive: compact tactical signals, succinct claims, and information arranged as a clear narrative.
2. Use tension through black space, bright white content planes, and the signature green as a signal—not decoration.
3. Echo the pitch with fine technical lines, coordinate marks, and disciplined asymmetry.
4. Let product proof lead: visuals and product outcomes should feel more prominent than generic technology claims.

### Color Philosophy
Near-black establishes focus and on-pitch intensity; white creates periods of clarity and credibility; the supplied brand’s electric green denotes active intelligence, confidence, and forward movement. The green will remain rare enough to retain meaning.

### Layout Paradigm
The site is constructed as a left-aligned analysis timeline: a narrow vertical signal rail anchors key sections while content breaks into asymmetric editorial compositions. The hero places the platform claim beside a large tactical ‘live signal’ panel rather than centering everything inside a conventional container.

### Signature Elements
1. A recurring vertical green signal rail with metric markers.
2. Fine pitch-coordinate grids and moving analytical paths.
3. High-contrast product windows using measured borders, not generic rounded cards.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a precise analysis tool: subtle line activation on hover, product cards reveal an explicit next outcome, and navigation moves purposefully to the next section. No decorative motion that competes with content.

### Animation
Use a 180–280ms custom ease-out for button feedback, panel raises, and line revelations. Animated route paths and scanning bands appear only in the hero and system diagram, respecting reduced-motion preferences. Content enters with short staggered opacity/translate transitions, never exaggerated scaling.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for headlines, navigation, labels, and numerical data because its angular geometry supports the technical sports tone. Use **Manrope** for supporting paragraphs due to its clarity at small sizes. Headlines are tightly tracked, oversized and left aligned; labels are uppercase with wide tracking.

### Brand Essence
**Tactivision.ai gives ambitious football teams autonomous, match-ready intelligence—before the first whistle.**

Personality adjectives: **decisive, exact, ambitious**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, outcome-led, and slightly provocative. CTAs sound like a serious invitation to operate differently, while microcopy remains concise and evidence oriented.

Example lines: “Know their next move before they make it.”  
“Turn every match into a decisive advantage.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied Tactivision.ai mark as the definitive wordmark. In the UI, pair it with a bold, text-free angular ‘T’ insignia at a confident scale; no substitute default-font logotype will be used.

### Signature Brand Color
**Tacti Signal Green — #63F58D**.

## Style Decisions

The vertical signal rail is mandatory on every major section, using section progression, metric ticks, and occasional active green markers to create a single tactical briefing timeline. Tacti Signal Green remains a precision signal color for primary actions, live states, key outcomes, and analytical paths; it is not used as a broad promotional field. Tactivision.ai identity appears as an angular T insignia with a supplied-wordmark reference in the footer and never as a default-font logo treatment.

For the refined public experience, the signal rail is intentionally quiet and structural rather than decorative. Light sections contain only the minimum tactical geometry needed to preserve the match-room feel, while green is limited to active states, calls to action, core metrics, and a single decisive word in each main headline.

Each major section now connects to the briefing spine through a numbered marker, active node, and measured continuation. Product cards use a compact decision-output fragment to make the platform’s value feel operational rather than feature-list driven.

## Product positioning source notes

**Opposition Analyst** is the autonomous, flagship multi-agent system. Its intended customer value is proactive pre-match briefings covering formations, pressing triggers, patterns, weaknesses, visual maps, cited data, and confidence signals.

**TactiScout** is the recruitment-intelligence layer. It is positioned around player discovery, Tactical Twin matching, similarity scoring, market-value context, and an organised recruitment pipeline.

**Vision Engine** is a forthcoming computer-vision product. It is positioned around converting available match video into structured, decision-ready event intelligence, expanding analysis to leagues and academies that lack conventional data coverage.
