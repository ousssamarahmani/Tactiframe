# Playvision reference review

Reviewed https://www.withplayvision.ai/ on 17 August 2026. The visible reference uses a dark, cinematic sports-analysis hero with a large player silhouette, coordinate labels, tracking boxes, and a headline about AI/computer vision. Its product narrative is organized around three ideas: frame-by-frame movement tracking, film transformed into searchable insights, and analytics presented as a polished product surface. The page is basketball-focused and also includes transfer-portal, player-ranking, pricing, and roster-evaluation language.

Tactivision.ai intentionally does not reproduce the reference's hero composition, copy, basketball imagery, transfer-portal framing, or UI structure. Instead, the new Vision Engine layer uses the existing Tactivision football still as a bounded product surface and adds an original, restrained motion system: five green tracking markers, two tactical route traces, a travelling ball point, and a compact `LIVE TRACK / 06 PLAYERS` readout. The animation supports Tactivision's own narrative of video input, structured player/event signals, AI-assisted interpretation, and coach-ready output. It is labelled as in development and avoids unsupported performance claims.
